# Welcome

**Welcome to Fewer.**

Fewer is a focused work search. Instead of blasting applications, you pick a small set of companies where you’d do genuinely good work, find warm paths to people who can hire you, and build real conversations.

**What sets it apart**

- **We don’t lead with applications.** Your resume is a closing document, not an opening one.
- **Quality over quantity.** We cap active conversations so you focus on companies you genuinely want to pursue.
- **You remain in control.** I help with research, structure, and drafts. You decide and send.
- **Warm paths over cold spam.** Most outreach goes through connectors, not strangers.

**Process**

1. **Orient:** short chat; connect Notion (~10 min)
2. **Profile:** interview about work you enjoy (~30 min, but pause anytime)
3. **Criteria:** lock search preferences (~30 min)
4. **Company pack:** Michael sources scored companies (you wait)
5. **Choose companies:** rate the shortlist (paced)
6. Find people → start conversations → develop relationships (~30 min per day)

**Privacy**

You’ll work in a shared ChatGPT project so Michael can improve the system across pilots, and your durable state (profile, criteria, companies, people, conversations) will live in Notion which ChatGPT will access. Your chats and Notion pack are private to you and Michael. He may read your chats only to improve the system, but you can delete any chat or content in Notion at any time. You’ll stay in control of every decision and every message that goes out.

# How to get started

You’ll need a paid ChatGPT account because the free tier’s context window is too small for this workflow. ChatGPT Go ($8/month) is enough, you don’t need Plus or Pro.

Michael will send you three invites: one to your private Fewer pack in Notion, one to [Slack](https://fewer-app.slack.com), and one to a shared ChatGPT project.

When the ChatGPT invite arrives, accept it, open the project, start a new chat, and say “Let’s begin.” That’s the only step you need to take to start. Connecting Notion and joining Slack support the work you’ll do in that chat.

# The process

Roughly six phases plus one offline research step. You talk in ChatGPT; durable state lives in your Notion pack.

| Phase | Description |
|---|---|
| Phase 0 — Orient | Connect Notion, get the lay of the land, ask whatever you need about the approach.<br><br>**Your effort:** A short orientation chat. No homework. |
| Phase 1 — Understand you | An interview about the work you enjoy, your strengths, what energizes and drains you, and what you want next.<br><br>**Your effort:** An honest conversation. No tests or take-homes.<br>**Deliverable:** Profile and Voice pages in Notion |
| Phase 2 — Define your search | Turn that profile into practical criteria: industries, company size and stage, roles, geography and hours, compensation, and other non-negotiables.<br><br>**Your effort:** Discuss preferences and decide trade-offs.<br>**Deliverable:** Search Criteria page in Notion |
| Offline company research | Michael sources and pre-scores companies against your criteria, then loads them into your Companies database. The chat does not invent a company list.<br><br>**Your effort:** Wait for the company pack.<br>**Deliverable:** A shortlist worth reviewing |
| Phase 3 — Choose companies | Review companies one by one. For each: Select, Reject, or Unsure, and say why.<br><br>**Your effort:** Thoughtful decisions, not rubber-stamping.<br>**Deliverable:** A rated Companies database |
| Phase 4 — Find people | Identify people connected to your selected companies (LinkedIn, your network, second-degree intros, former employees).<br><br>**Your effort:** Bring names and context. You decide who’s worth approaching.<br>**Deliverable:** People rows in Notion |
| Phase 5 — Start conversations | Draft personal, low-pressure messages in your natural voice — to learn about the company and someone’s experience, not to ask strangers for a job.<br><br>**Your effort:** Review, edit, and send yourself.<br>**Deliverable:** Outreach tracked in Conversations |
| Phase 6 — Develop relationships | Follow-ups, replies, introductions, conversation prep, notes on what you learned, and who to approach next.<br><br>**Your effort:** Have real conversations, follow up, and decide which paths deserve more energy.<br>**Deliverable:** A live pipeline of relationships and opportunities |

# Time and effort

The aim is a searchable practice you can sustain, not a second full-time job.

A reasonable target is about **30 minutes a day**. Some days take more when you’re in live conversations or reviewing companies. Early phases (profile and criteria) are mostly chat. Later phases need more of your judgment and outbound effort.

Success looks like **hiring-manager conversations**, not applications sent or LinkedIn connections accumulated.

| What do I need to bring? | Willingness to talk honestly about your work, preferences you can live with, and later: names from your network plus time to send and follow up on messages. |
| Who is this for? | People who want a high-signal, relationship-first search and are willing to trade spray-and-pray volume for focus. |
